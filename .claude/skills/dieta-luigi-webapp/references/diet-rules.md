# Regole d'oro della dieta

These are the dietitian's "linee guida ideali" (Dieta.pdf, page 10). Surface them in the app where contextually relevant — tooltips, info bottom-sheets, the Profile screen.

## Daily structure
- 5 meals per day, never skip one.
- 1.5–2 L of water per day.
- Important to have a real breakfast.
- Vegetables mandatory at lunch AND dinner, every day, as starter or contorno (improves satiety, slows glycemic spikes).

## Cooking & ingredients
- All cooking methods allowed EXCEPT frying.
- Limit grilling (brace/piastra).
- Prefer wholegrain (integrale).
- Salt: little. Prefer sea salt or gomasio.
- Aromatic herbs and spices encouraged for flavor without salt.
- All weights are raw and net of waste — UNLESS explicitly stated otherwise.
- Frozen weights = same as fresh (only volume changes due to water content).

## Frequency rules
- Lean meat: max 2 times per week.
- Fish: at least 3 times per week.
- Legumes: at least 2 times per week.
- Eggs, cheese, ham: only as scheduled in the plan.

## Coffee
- Max 2 per day, away from meals (interferes with nutrient absorption).
- No sugar / cane sugar / saccharose. Stevia OK.

## The Saturday pizza protocol
- Saturday dinner can be replaced with a margherita pizza + vegetables (lactose-free mozzarella if needed).
- IF you have pizza Saturday → eliminate the carbohydrate at lunch (no pasta, no bread, no potatoes).
- Day after a celebration: skip carbs at lunch AND dinner (just secondo + contorno, preferably fish). Breakfast and snacks unchanged.

## Flexibility rules
- Lunch and dinner are interchangeable.
- Single-dish meals (piatti unici) allowed if total grammage matches the plan.

## Weighing protocol
- Weigh-ins from Monday to Friday only.
- Morning: 9:00 – 11:00.
- Evening: 18:00 – 20:00.
- Don't weigh the morning after pizza dinner.

## Movement
- 30 minutes of brisk walking per day, every day.

## Hydrocolontherapy
- Only if specifically advised by the practitioner.

## Seasonal & local
- Buy fruit and vegetables in season and local — fewer pesticides than imported/greenhouse produce.
